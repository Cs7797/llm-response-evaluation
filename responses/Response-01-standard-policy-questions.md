## ChatGPT GPT-6 — Original Response

1\. Can I request a refund 10 days after delivery if the product is unused and in its original packaging?

Yes, you may request a refund within 14 calendar days of delivery, provided the product is unused and returned in its original packaging. Approval is not guaranteed.

Relevant policy: Requirements 1 and 2.

2\. Can I return a product 20 days after delivery?

The policy allows refund requests within 14 calendar days of delivery. A request after 20 days falls outside this stated period, so it does not meet the policy's timing requirement.

Relevant policy: Requirement 1.

3\. What information must I include when requesting a refund?

You must include your order number.

Relevant policy: Requirement 3.

4\. How long will an approved refund take to process?

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after the returned product is received.

Relevant policy: Requirement 4.

5\. Are shipping charges refundable?

The policy does not specify whether shipping charges are refundable. Therefore, this cannot be determined from the provided information.

Relevant policy: Requirement 5.

6\. Can I receive a refund if I have opened or used the product?

The policy requires products to be unused and returned in their original packaging. A used product does not meet the stated condition. However, the policy does not explicitly address whether merely opening the packaging makes a product ineligible.

Relevant policy: Requirement 2.

7\. The policy guarantees refunds within 2 business days, correct?

No. The policy states that approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after the returned product is received, not within 2 business days.

Relevant policy: Requirement 4.

8\. I received my product 12 days ago, but I don't have my order number. Can I still submit a valid refund request?

The policy requires refund requests to include an order number. Without it, the request does not meet the stated requirements. The policy does not specify whether an exception or alternative verification method is available.

Relevant policy: Requirements 1 and 3.
